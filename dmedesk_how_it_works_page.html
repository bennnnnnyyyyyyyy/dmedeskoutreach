Onboarding

Seven days to go live

Our team does the heavy lifting. Most clients receive real patient calls within one week of signing.

1
Day 1 — Kickoff Call (60 min)

We learn your operation

Our specialist walks through your call volume, products, intake workflow, and handoff requirements. This call drives everything.

  • Map your intake questions and required fields
  • Identify which calls AI handles vs. transfers to staff
  • Confirm where completed intake details should go
  • Sign your HIPAA Business Associate Agreement
2
Days 2–3 — Build

We configure your AI agent

Custom intake script built for your products. Workflow handoffs configured. Data is prepared for your team before you touch anything.

  • Custom script for your products (orthotics, CPAP, mobility, etc.)
  • Workflow handoffs configured for completed intake data
  • PHI routing set up through encrypted HIPAA-compliant pipeline
  • Live transfer rules configured for your team's numbers
3
Day 4 — Phone Setup

Your number, zero disruption

We connect DMEDesk to your existing phone number. Patients keep calling the same number they already know.

  • SIP forwarding from your existing landline or VoIP number
  • Optional: provision a new US number
  • After-hours and holiday schedules configured
  • Overflow rules for when live transfer is unavailable
4
Day 5 — QA

We test before your patients do

Our team runs a full QA session — 20+ real scenarios, intake data verification, and escalation path testing.

  • 20+ test scenarios against your intake script
  • Verify intake data appears correctly in the agreed workflow
  • Confirm live transfer connects to the right person
  • Review recordings and transcripts in your dashboard
5
Days 6–7 — Go Live

DMEDesk starts answering calls

We flip the switch. Our team monitors live for the first 48 hours and makes real-time adjustments.

  • Live monitoring for the first 48 hours
  • Same-day script adjustments from real call data
  • Staff briefed on the transfer experience
  • Weekly check-in call scheduled for the first month
