What is DMEDesk?
Learn about DMEDesk — the AI voice receptionist platform built exclusively for DMEPOS suppliers.

DMEDesk is an AI-powered voice receptionist platform purpose-built for DMEPOS (Durable Medical Equipment, Prosthetics, Orthotics, and Supplies) suppliers. It uses advanced AI to handle real-time inbound patient calls and automate outbound coordination calls — so your staff can focus on fulfillment instead of phone queues.

Core Features

  • Inbound Patient Intake — The AI answers calls 24/7, verifies patient identity, collects insurance info, and routes to the right team member.
  • Outbound Follow-Up Calls — Automate prior authorization follow-ups, prescription chasing, and patient notifications via outbound AI call campaigns.
  • Workflow Integration — Configurable workflow handoffs for your intake and operations process.
  • HIPAA-Compliant Infrastructure — All call data is encrypted. A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is included with every account.

Key Benefits

  • 24/7 Availability — Never miss a patient call again. DMEDesk answers every call, even after hours.
  • Scalability — One number can handle unlimited parallel inbound calls during peak hours.
  • Compliance-First Design — Built with HIPAA, TCPA, and CMS requirements baked in from the ground up.
  • Faster Prior Auth — Automate follow-up calls to payers and physicians to reduce approval cycle time.

Why DMEDesk?

Most AI voice platforms are generic. DMEDesk is the only AI receptionist trained specifically on DMEPOS workflows — including HCPCS code intake, LCD criteria, Medicare/Medicaid compliance, and prior authorization processes.

How It Works
Understand how DMEDesk processes and handles live patient phone conversations using AI.

DMEDesk blends AI speech recognition with LLM-based response generation to conduct real-time phone conversations. Here is the simplified flow for both inbound and outbound calls:

1

Call is initiated

Inbound: A patient dials your assigned DMEDesk number. Outbound: DMEDesk dials a list of contacts from your campaign (payers, physicians, patients).

2

Speech Recognition

The AI listens to what the caller says and transcribes it into text in real time using high-accuracy speech-to-text.

3

LLM Decision

DMEDesk uses your configured system prompt and knowledge base to craft the appropriate reply or decide the next action (verify patient, collect info, transfer call).

4

Voice Output

The AI's response is converted to natural-sounding speech by DMEDesk AI voice and played back to the caller in real time.

5

Actions & Data

After or during the call, intake details are prepared, transcripts are stored, and automations are triggered.

Inbound vs. Outbound

  • Inbound: Patient dials your number → AI picks up → greets, verifies, collects intake info → routes or escalates as needed.
  • Outbound: You upload a lead list or sync from an approved source → AI dials each contact → follows your call script → logs outcome and triggers follow-up.

Key Terminology
Essential terms you need to know to use the DMEDesk platform effectively.

🤖 AI Receptionist / Agent

The AI entity you configure to handle inbound or outbound calls. Defined by a system prompt, voice, and set of tools.

📣 Campaign (Outbound)

A batch of outbound calls to a list of contacts — payers, physicians, or patients. Can be launched manually or triggered automatically.

☎️ Inbound Number

A dedicated phone number assigned to your AI receptionist. Patients call this number and the AI answers.

📝 System Prompt

The instruction set that defines how your AI receptionist behaves — what it says, how it responds, what it collects, and when it escalates.

📊 Post-Call Variables

Structured data extracted after each call (e.g., patient_name, insurance_id, call_outcome) used in automations and workflow handoffs.

🔗 Workflow Integration

A configurable handoff between DMEDesk and your operational workflow for routing patient records and call data.

📚 Knowledge Base

Documents (PDFs, text files) your AI can reference during calls to answer questions about products, insurance, or processes.

🔗 Webhook

A URL endpoint that receives data when events occur (call ended, appointment booked) to trigger actions in external systems.

Treatment, Payment, and Operations intake — the HIPAA-compliant process for collecting patient information at the start of a new DME order.

🔒 BAA

Business Associate Agreement — a HIPAA-required contract between DMEDesk and your organization that governs how PHI is handled.

System Prompts
AI Receptionist › System Prompts
System Prompts
The system prompt is the instruction set that defines your AI Receptionist's behavior.

What is a System Prompt?

The system prompt is a plain-text (or structured) instruction set you write to tell the AI how to behave during calls. It defines the greeting, what information to collect, how to handle edge cases, and when to transfer or end the call.

Best Practices

  • Use objective-based prompting — tell the AI what outcome to achieve, not just what to say word for word.
  • Always include an AI disclosure in the opening line (required by law in many states).
  • Define clear escalation criteria — when should the AI transfer to a human?
  • Keep prompts focused. One AI per use case (intake, prior auth, patient notification) performs better than one AI trying to do everything.
  • Never include raw PHI in the system prompt itself.

Example Structure

  • Identity: Who the AI is and what practice it represents
  • Disclosure: AI identification statement
  • Objective: What the AI should accomplish on this call
  • Data Collection: What fields to gather from the caller
  • Escalation Rules: When and how to transfer
  • Closing: How to end the call professionally

Voice Selection
Choose the right voice for your AI Receptionist to match your practice's brand and patient expectations.

Built-in Voice Library

DMEDesk includes a library of professional, natural-sounding voices optimized for healthcare phone interactions. Voices vary by accent, tone, gender, and speed.

Choosing a Voice

  • Use a neutral, professional tone for intake and clinical calls.
  • Avoid overly upbeat or sales-like voices for patient-facing healthcare calls.
  • Preview each voice with a sample script before assigning it.
  • Consider your patient demographics — some patient populations respond better to certain accents or speech rates.

Voice Speed & Filler Audio

You can adjust speech rate for each voice. Filler audio (brief "uh-huh", "I see", "one moment") can be enabled to make pauses sound more natural during processing delays.

TPO Intake
Inbound Calls › TPO Intake
TPO Intake
HIPAA-compliant Treatment, Payment, and Operations intake for new DME patients.

HIPAA's Privacy Rule permits the use and disclosure of PHI for Treatment, Payment, and Operations (TPO) without specific patient authorization. The DMEDesk AI is configured to collect patient information within the scope of TPO.

TPO Disclosure Script

Your system prompt should include a standard TPO disclosure. Example opening: "This call is for treatment, payment, and operations purposes related to your durable medical equipment order. All information you share is protected under HIPAA."

What can be collected under TPO

  • Patient identity and contact information
  • Insurance and order information
  • Physician and referral information
  • Diagnosis and equipment information relevant to the order
⚠️

Do not configure your AI to collect information beyond what is necessary for the specific TPO purpose. Minimize PHI collection to what is required.

Prior Auth Follow-Up
Outbound Calls › Prior Auth Follow-Up
Prior Authorization Follow-Up
Automate outbound calls to payers to check prior authorization status.

Prior authorization follow-up is one of the highest-value use cases for DMEDesk. The AI calls payers on your behalf, navigates phone trees (using DTMF when needed), and collects authorization status, approval numbers, or denial reasons.

Setting Up Prior Auth Calls

  • Create an outbound receptionist with a prompt focused on prior auth status checks.
  • Include payer-specific phone tree navigation if needed (DTMF key sequences).
  • Define post-call variables to capture: auth status, auth number, denial code, follow-up date.
  • Map post-call variables to your agreed workflow fields.

Best Practices

  • Schedule prior auth calls during payer business hours (typically 8 AM — 5 PM local time).
  • Set a maximum of 3 retry attempts per contact before flagging for manual follow-up.
  • Review transcripts daily for calls where the AI was unable to navigate the phone tree.

HIPAA & Protected Health Information
Your obligations and DMEDesk's safeguards under HIPAA.

DMEDesk is designed from the ground up for HIPAA-covered entities. As a DMEPOS supplier, you are a Covered Entity under HIPAA and are responsible for ensuring all systems handling PHI — including DMEDesk — are compliant.

  • Train staff on appropriate use of call recordings and transcripts.
  • Report suspected breaches to DMEDesk within 24 hours of discovery.

  • AES-256 encryption at rest for all call data and transcripts
  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) on all dashboard access
  • Audit logging for all PHI access events
  • Configurable data retention with automatic purging
  • No PHI used for AI model training

TCPA & Consent
Compliance › TCPA & Consent
TCPA & Consent
Legal requirements for outbound AI voice calls under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act.

The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) governs outbound automated and AI-generated voice calls in the United States. Non-compliance can result in fines of $500–$1,500 per call.

Key Requirements

  • Prior Express Consent — You must have written or express consent from contacts before placing automated AI calls to them.
  • Opt-Out Mechanism — All outbound calls must provide a way for recipients to opt out. DMEDesk includes a built-in opt-out phrase detection.
  • Do-Not-Call List — Scrub your contact lists against the National DNC Registry and maintain an internal DNC list.
  • Calling Hours — Calls may only be placed between 8 AM and 9 PM local time of the recipient.
⚠️

DMEDesk provides the technology. You are solely responsible for ensuring consent and TCPA compliance for your contact lists. Consult legal counsel for compliance guidance specific to your operations.

AI Disclosure Rules
Compliance › AI Disclosure Rules
AI Disclosure Rules
State and federal requirements for disclosing AI identity during phone calls.

Several U.S. states have enacted laws requiring AI voice agents to disclose their non-human nature at the start of a call. DMEDesk includes a built-in AI disclosure option, but you are responsible for ensuring your system prompt complies.

Key State Requirements

  • Texas SB 140 — Requires AI disclosure within the first 30 seconds of a call.
  • California — BOT Disclosure Act requires disclosure at the start of interactions.
  • Federal FTC Guidance — Deceptive AI impersonation of humans may violate FTC Act Section 5.

Recommended Disclosure Language

Include in your system prompt's opening: "Hi, this is [Name], an AI assistant calling on behalf of [Practice Name]. I'm not a human — I'm here to help with [purpose of call]."

💡

DMEDesk's default system prompt templates include compliant AI disclosure language for all supported states.

CMS & Accreditation
Compliance › CMS & Accreditation
CMS & Accreditation
How DMEDesk fits into CMS requirements for DMEPOS suppliers.

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) requires DMEPOS suppliers to maintain accreditation and comply with supplier standards. DMEDesk is a tool to support your operations — it does not replace your accreditation obligations.

Key CMS Considerations

  • AI phone systems used for patient intake must be interactive and capable of routing to a live person — DMEDesk supports this via call transfer.
  • Annual accreditation surveys may review your patient contact and intake processes. Ensure your AI scripts align with your accreditation body's standards.
  • Prior authorization requirements expanded in April 2026 to include seven additional HCPCS codes. DMEDesk can be configured to handle prior auth intake for all affected codes.

Data Retention
Configure how long call recordings, transcripts, and patient data are stored.

Default Retention Policy

By default, DMEDesk retains call recordings and transcripts for 90 days. After this period, recordings are permanently deleted. Transcripts and call metadata are retained for 1 year.

Custom Retention Settings

Enterprise customers can configure custom retention periods. Go to Settings → Data Retention to adjust the retention period for recordings, transcripts, and metadata.

Manual Deletion

Individual calls can be manually deleted from your dashboard at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

⚠️

Ensure your data retention settings align with your HIPAA obligations and any applicable state health data retention laws (typically 6–7 years for medical records).

AI Behavior Issues
Troubleshooting › AI Behavior Issues
AI Behavior Issues
Diagnose and fix unexpected AI behavior during calls.

AI goes off-script

Cause: System prompt is too vague or doesn't cover the scenario the caller triggered.
Fix: Add explicit instructions for the scenario. Use objective-based prompting and add guardrails: "If the caller asks anything not related to [topic], politely redirect to [purpose]."

AI collects wrong information

Cause: Field names or collection order in the prompt is ambiguous.
Fix: Be explicit about each field name, format, and order. Example: "Ask for the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format."

AI doesn't transfer when it should

Cause: Transfer trigger conditions in the prompt are not specific enough.
Fix: Add clear, specific transfer triggers. Example: "If the caller says they want to speak to a human, immediately use the transfer tool."

AI repeats itself

Cause: The system prompt has conflicting instructions or the AI is looping on an unanswered question.
Fix: Review for conflicting instructions. Add: "If the caller does not answer after two attempts, offer to call them back and end the call."

Call Quality
Troubleshooting › Call Quality
Call Quality Issues
Resolve audio, latency, and connection issues.

Choppy or robotic audio

Cause: Network instability between DMEDesk's voice infrastructure and the caller's carrier.
Fix: Check if the issue is isolated to one carrier or number. Test from different devices and locations. Contact support if the issue persists across multiple callers.

High latency (long pauses before AI responds)

Cause: LLM processing time or slow STT transcription.
Fix: Enable Filler Audio to fill processing pauses naturally. Simplify your system prompt — longer, more complex prompts increase processing time.

Call drops unexpectedly

Cause: Telephony timeout or silence detection triggering early call end.
Fix: Review the call transcript to see where the drop occurred. Adjust silence timeout settings in your receptionist configuration.

Integration Issues
Troubleshooting › Integration Issues
Integration Issues
Resolve problems with workflow handoffs, webhooks, and API connections.

Data not appearing in the expected workflow

Check: Integration credentials are valid. Field mappings are correctly configured. The post-call handoff is enabled for the receptionist.

Webhook not receiving data

Check: Webhook URL is correct and accessible over HTTPS. Your endpoint returns a 200 response. Check your server logs for incoming POST requests from DMEDesk's IP range.

API authentication errors

Check: Your API key is active and has the correct permissions. API keys can be regenerated in Settings → API.

Patient lookup returning no results

Check: The patient exists in the approved source with matching data. The lookup fields (MBI, DOB, name) match exactly — even whitespace can cause mismatches.
