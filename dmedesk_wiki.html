Dialer Battlecard

Your quick-reference helper for cold calling DME practice owners and booking demos.

The 30-Second Hook

"Most DME suppliers lose 30% of their new patient revenue to voicemail or hold times. We provide an AI Front Desk that's natively trained on DME workflows, answers 24/7, and writes directly into your PMS so you never miss a referral again."

Our Goal as Openers

Remember: your goal on the call is **NOT** to sell the software or close a contract. Your goal is simply to **reveal basic operational pain** and get them excited enough to spend 15 minutes checking out a live demonstration.

The Three Discovery Camps

Every DME owner you talk to falls into one of these camps. Find out which one quickly:

Camp A: Voicemail Bleed

Patients call, hit voicemail, and call the next supplier. They are actively losing cash.
Key Question: *"How many new patient referrals are you losing because they hit voicemail?"*

Camp B: Staff Burnout

Staff is drowning in manual resupply requests and chasing doctors for prior auths.
Key Question: *"What if your team never had to chase a doctor for a signature again?"*

Camp C: Full Replacement

Looking to replace their front-line office staff entirely with AI. This is out of scope!
Action: Redirect them to the hybrid overflow model.

What Happens in the Live Demo?

💡 How to Sell the 15-Minute Demo

When inviting prospects, tell them exactly what to expect. It's safe, visual, and highly practical:

  • The Live Call Test: The closer triggers our voice AI to call the prospect's mobile phone live during the meeting so they can experience the sub-second response latency themselves.
  • PMS Sync Walkthrough: We show how the AI writes orders and notes directly into their database (Brightree or NikoHealth) in real-time.
  • Custom Roadmap & Pricing: We draft a customized onboarding roadmap tailored to their office overflow volume.

Hook Questions

Questions designed to help rookie BDO openers gather basic info and spark demo interest.

Gathering Basic Discovery Info

To qualify the lead for our closers, try to gather these basic details naturally during the chat:

  • How they currently manage after-hours calls or overflow during peak hours (voicemail, an answering service, or just in-house staff).
  • Which **Practice Management System (PMS)** software they use (e.g. Brightree, NikoHealth, Bonafide).
  • Approximate monthly referrals volume to estimate lost cash.

Type 1: "Voicemail Bleed" Questions

The After-Hours Question

"How many referrals do you think you lose each month because they hit a voicemail box after 5:00 PM?"

The Hold-Time Dropout

"When patients wait on hold for more than 4 minutes, they usually hang up and call a competitor. How is your staff managing overflow?"

Type 2: "Staff Burnout" Questions

Repetitive Chasing Call Pain

"What percentage of your team's day is completely wasted chasing doctor offices for missing signatures?"

The PA Bottleneck

"What is your current turnaround time from receiving a referral to securing a clean prior authorization?"

The 'Magic Wand' Hook

"If you could have an automated front desk helper that never called in sick, spoke 5 languages, and instantly synced eligibility records into Brightree... would that be worth a quick 15-minute look next Tuesday?"

Objection Handler

The exact, simple responses for the 7 most common objections rookie SDRs face while cold calling.

1. "Patients hate talking to robots."

Expand
Playbook Response
"Actually, patients hate waiting. Right now, they hit voicemail or hold. DMEDesk gives them an instant, friendly answer and books their intake immediately."

Why it works: It pivots from "robot" to "speed". Patients prefer getting help immediately over sitting on hold or speaking to an answering machine.

2. "We already have automated text reminders."

Expand
Playbook Response
"Text reminders are passive one-way notifications. DMEDesk is a live, two-way helper that answers patient questions and updates your PMS in real-time."

Why it works: Clearly outlines the difference between passive texts and fully integrated active voice receptionists.

3. "What happens if there is a medical emergency?"

Expand
Playbook Response
"Emergency and life-support calls are instantly flagged by the AI and routed to your on-call human staff. We never handle critical emergencies with AI alone."

Why it works: Establishes absolute trust and compliance safety immediately. Calmly reassures them.

4. "Setup and PMS integration takes too much effort."

Expand
Playbook Response
"We handle 100% of the heavy lifting. We assign a dedicated success manager for a 2-week White-Glove onboarding, at zero extra cost."

Why it works: Knocks down the friction of implementation, placing the work load entirely on our team.

5. "What if the AI is not a good fit and we cancel?"

Expand
Playbook Response
"We operate month-to-month with no long-term contracts. Plus, we include a 30-day money-back guarantee with zero cancellation fees."

Why it works: Removes all buyer risk, making a demo booking a safe and logical next step.

6. "We are way too small for complex AI."

Expand
Playbook Response
"Our after-hours model costs less than one missed patient referral. If DMEDesk recovers just 2 patient dropouts a month, it completely pays for itself."

Why it works: Shifts their focus from "monthly tool overhead" to "recovering lost patient revenue".

7. "We need to discuss this internally first."

Expand
Playbook Response
"Of course. But since every week you wait is another week of missed referrals, let's book a quick 15-minute live call test so you can show the team exactly how it sounds."

Why it works: Re-establishes the urgency of the "voicemail leak" and guides them back to a simple, visual next step.

DME Niche Glossary

Every acronym and term explained in simple, plain English so you sound like an expert.

HCPCS Codes

Standard medical billing codes used for equipment (e.g. E1390 for an Oxygen Concentrator). Knowing these helps reps speak professionally with suppliers.

LCD (Local Coverage Determination)

The exact medical criteria Medicare uses to decide if they will pay for a piece of equipment. If criteria aren't met, Medicare rejects the claim.

Prior Auth (Prior Authorization)

Approval required from an insurance company before equipment can be delivered. Chasing auths takes massive staff hours.

PMS (Practice Management System)

Database software DME suppliers use to track patients, orders, and billing (e.g. Brightree, NikoHealth). DMEDesk syncs with these automatically.

MBI (Medicare Beneficiary Identifier)

The patient's unique Medicare card number. Our voice AI can read and verify this ID live over the phone.

PHI (Protected Health Information)

Any private patient details (names, dates of birth, medical histories) that are legally protected under HIPAA.

BAA (Business Associate Agreement)

A HIPAA-required contract between DMEDesk and the practice that guarantees patient data is handled securely and legally.

TPO (Treatment, Payment, and Operations)

The HIPAA rule allowing providers to collect patient data for treatment or billing without requiring written patient consent.

NPI (National Provider Identifier)

A unique 10-digit number assigned to US healthcare providers. The AI looks this up to verify doctors live on calls.

ICD-10 Codes

Standardized diagnosis codes used by doctors to document a patient's medical condition on prescriptions.

System Prompt

The set of instructions that tells the AI receptionist how to behave, what questions to ask, and when to transfer calls.

Knowledge Base

Supporting documents (PDFs, instruction sheets) that the AI references mid-call to answer patient questions instantly.

Warm Transfer

Handing a caller over to a human agent after the AI speaks to the staff member first and introduces the patient.

Cold Transfer

Handing a caller over directly to a human staff member's queue without any introduction.

STT (Speech-to-Text) & TTS (Text-to-Speech)

The technologies the AI uses to transcribe spoken words to text, and to generate natural voice answers back.

Conversational Latency

The delay before the AI replies. DMEDesk operates at sub-second latency (under 1 second) so calls feel like natural talk.

Webhook

A secure digital bridge that automatically sends call outcomes, data, and transcripts to other software after a call ends.

Filler Audio

Natural spoken sounds (like "uh-huh" or "one moment") played by the AI to make brief processing pauses feel human.

Outbound Campaign

An automated batch of outbound phone calls placed by the AI to a specific list of contacts (payers or patients).

CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services)

The federal agency that regulates medical equipment billing, documentation requirements, and accreditation standards.

How It Works

The simple 5-step flow of our technology from a ring to PMS record.

1. Patient dials or Campaigns trigger

Patient calls the DME local office. Overflow, busy queues, or after-hours calls route directly to the custom DMEDesk voice receptionist.

2. Voice is parsed in real-time

3. Local DME rules applied

HIPAA & TCPA Compliance Made Simple

The critical rules you must know to reassure clients and stay legally safe.

1. What is HIPAA? (Medical Privacy)

Medicare and medical offices operate under incredibly strict privacy laws. Reassure prospects with these three facts:

  • Full BAA Coverage: We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every account, legally securing patient data.
  • Military-Grade Encryption: All voice recordings, transcripts, and PMS notes are encrypted using AES-256 at rest.
  • Safe Audit Trail: Complete history of every AI call is kept for legal accreditation.

2. TCPA Compliance (Outbound Rules)

⚠️

The Critical 5-Second Disclosure Rule

Every outbound AI receptionist call must identify itself as an automated AI helper within the **first 5 seconds** of the call. Failing to do this violates TCPA state laws and carries heavy penalties.

3. Consent and Do Not Call (DNC)

We only run automated outbound campaigns to patients who have active consent forms signed with their DMEPOS supplier. We sync with local DNC files automatically.

Your Background ➔ BDR

How to translate Doctor Chasing and Prior Auth coordination into elite BDR opener skills.

🎯 The Golden Opener Mindset

As an Opener BDR, you do NOT need to sell the entire software suite, explain pricing, or coordinate technical integrations. Your primary goal is to identify a bottleneck (voicemail bleed or staff burnout) and book a 15-minute demo with our closer team. Stay focused on simple, high-impact conversations!

Translating Your Specialist Skills

Your previous experience in clinical administration is a massive competitive advantage. Here is how your skills map directly to sales outreach:

📞 Provider Coordination ➔ Gatekeeper Mastery

Old Duty: Engaging doctor offices to secure documentation.
New Skill: You already know the clinical language and how medical front offices operate. When you speak to a gatekeeper, you sound like a peer, not a generic telemarketer. Use that confidence to explain we help ease administrative burdens.

⏱️ Claims & Auth Management ➔ Persistence & Follow-up

Old Duty: Following up on outstanding requests and approvals.
New Skill: You understand that claims don't get approved without follow-ups. In outreach, 80% of meetings are booked on the 3rd to 5th touchpoint. Your persistence is your superpower here.

⚙️ Process Optimization ➔ ROI & Value Discovery

Old Duty: Identifying administrative delays to enhance workflow.
New Skill: You know exactly where office bottlenecks happen (e.g. staff spending hours calling doctors or patients sitting on hold). You can easily explain how DMEDesk solves these specific pains to recover lost referrals.

🔒 Regulatory Compliance ➔ Security Reassurance

Old Duty: Ensuring medical records meet strict compliance guidelines.
New Skill: You don't have to fear security questions. You can confidently explain that DMEDesk signs BAAs (Business Associate Agreements) and encrypts all patient data using military-grade standards.

Technical Jargon Made Easy

Prospects or closers will sometimes use technical terms. Here is how to speak about them in plain English:

🔌 APIs (Application Programming Interfaces)

What it is: A secure, automatic digital pipeline between two pieces of software.
Why it matters: Instead of office staff copying patient names and phone numbers manually from the voicemail to their database, the API allows DMEDesk to send that patient data instantly and automatically.
Soundbite for calls: "We connect directly via secure APIs to your software, meaning your staff won't have to copy-paste or enter anything manually."

🧩 MCP (Model Context Protocol)

What it is: A universal plug for AI tools.
Why it matters: Traditionally, connecting an AI helper to different clinic systems required building a completely custom, expensive integration from scratch. MCP allows our AI to securely "plug in" to verify calendars, billing codes, and intake sheets in seconds.
Soundbite for calls: "We use the new Model Context Protocol standard, which lets our voice AI securely look up schedules and verify codes without requiring custom software development."

🔒 HIPAA, PHI & BAA (Medical Security Protocol)

What they are: HIPAA is the federal medical privacy law. PHI (Protected Health Information) is any private patient details. A BAA (Business Associate Agreement) is a legal contract where we assume legal liability for protecting that data.
Why it matters: DME practices cannot legally share patient details with vendors who don't sign a BAA. We sign a BAA with every single customer.
Soundbite for calls: "We sign a standard BAA with every practice, and all calls and transcripts are fully HIPAA-compliant and AES-256 encrypted."

🗣️ Medical STT & TTS (Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech)

What it is: STT is the AI transcribing what a caller says. TTS is the AI talking back in a natural human voice.
Why it matters: General voice bots get confused by complex medical terminology, HCPCS equipment codes, and doctor names. DMEDesk uses clinical-grade models designed specifically for DME vocabulary.
Soundbite for calls: "Our models are custom-trained on DME speech, meaning the AI understands specific medical equipment codes and practitioner names that generic bots miss."

⚡ Conversational Latency (Response Time)

What it is: The brief silence between when a caller stops speaking and when the voice receptionist responds.
Why it matters: If latency is 2 to 3 seconds (like standard chatbot engines), the patient gets frustrated or thinks the line dropped. DMEDesk operates at sub-second latency (under 1 second), making it sound like a natural flow.
Soundbite for calls: "DMEDesk answers with sub-second response times, so the patient never feels like they are waiting on a slow robotic machine."

🔄 Two-Way PMS Sync (Write-Back)

What it is: The AI receptionist database action of searching records (Read) and typing new patient info (Write-Back) directly into their office software.
Why it matters: Many generic call programs only email a recording to staff. DMEDesk actually logs the intake ticket and schedules the referral directly in Brightree or NikoHealth automatically.
Soundbite for calls: "We don't just email you transcripts. The system writes the intake data and insurance eligibility details directly back into Brightree."

CRM vs EHR vs PMS

Understanding the three main software systems in medical practices without the confusion.

The Three Systems at a Glance

Medical practices use different databases for different departments. As a BDO, you must know what they do so you don't confuse them on calls.

1. CRM (Customer Relationship Management)

Purpose: Tracking prospects, leads, and sales conversations.
Who uses it: Sales teams (including you!). We use it to coordinate our calls, emails, and demos.
Examples: HubSpot, Salesforce, Apollo.io.
DME Sync: We do NOT sync patient records here. This is purely our internal sales notebook.

2. EHR / EMR (Electronic Health or Medical Record)

Purpose: Tracking medical history and clinical records.
Who uses it: Doctors, nurses, and clinical practitioners.
Examples: Epic, Athenahealth, Cerner, eClinicalWorks.
DME Sync: Read-only lookup. We reference the EHR to verify prescriptions and doctor notes.

3. PMS (Practice Management System)

Purpose: Office operations, patient intake, equipment scheduling, and billing.
Who uses it: Front desk coordinators, billers, and DME operators.
Examples: Brightree, NikoHealth.
DME Sync: Deep Integration. This is where DMEDesk writes patient intake details and eligibility ticks in real-time.

Comparison Matrix

System Type Primary Focus Main Users DMEDesk Integration
CRM Prospects, meetings & emails BDOs / Closers None (Used for sales track only)
EHR Diagnoses, charts & clinical notes Doctors / Clinicians Read-only (Prescription checking)
PMS Scheduling, inventory & billing Front Office Staff Deep Two-Way Sync (Brightree, Nikohealth)

🔑 The PMS Conversation Opener

When speaking to a DME practice manager, always ask: "Which Practice Management System are you running?"
Once they say Brightree or NikoHealth, reassure them: "Perfect, DMEDesk has a native integration with them, so we write intakes directly to your software without your team doing manual work."
