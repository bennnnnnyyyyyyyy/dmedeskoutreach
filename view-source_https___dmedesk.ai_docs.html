<div class="bc">Introduction <span>&rsaquo;</span> What is DMEDesk?</div>
<div class="pg-title">What is DMEDesk?</div>
<div class="pg-sub">Learn about DMEDesk &mdash; the AI voice receptionist platform built exclusively for DMEPOS suppliers.</div>
<p>DMEDesk is an AI-powered voice receptionist platform purpose-built for DMEPOS (Durable Medical Equipment, Prosthetics, Orthotics, and Supplies) suppliers. It uses advanced AI to handle real-time inbound patient calls and automate outbound coordination calls &mdash; so your staff can focus on fulfillment instead of phone queues.</p>
<h2>Core Features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Inbound Patient Intake</strong> &mdash; The AI answers calls 24/7, verifies patient identity, collects insurance info, and routes to the right team member.</li>
<li><strong>Outbound Follow-Up Calls</strong> &mdash; Automate prior authorization follow-ups, prescription chasing, and patient notifications via outbound AI call campaigns.</li>
<li><strong>Workflow Integration</strong> &mdash; Configurable workflow handoffs for your intake and operations process.</li>
<li><strong>HIPAA-Compliant Infrastructure</strong> &mdash; All call data is encrypted. A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is included with every account.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Key Benefits</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>24/7 Availability</strong> &mdash; Never miss a patient call again. DMEDesk answers every call, even after hours.</li>
<li><strong>Scalability</strong> &mdash; One number can handle unlimited parallel inbound calls during peak hours.</li>
<li><strong>Compliance-First Design</strong> &mdash; Built with HIPAA, TCPA, and CMS requirements baked in from the ground up.</li>
<li><strong>Faster Prior Auth</strong> &mdash; Automate follow-up calls to payers and physicians to reduce approval cycle time.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Why DMEDesk?</h2>
<p>Most AI voice platforms are generic. DMEDesk is the only AI receptionist trained specifically on DMEPOS workflows &mdash; including HCPCS code intake, LCD criteria, Medicare/Medicaid compliance, and prior authorization processes.</p>

<div class="bc">Introduction <span>&rsaquo;</span> How It Works</div>
<div class="pg-title">How It Works</div>
<div class="pg-sub">Understand how DMEDesk processes and handles live patient phone conversations using AI.</div>
<p>DMEDesk blends <strong>AI speech recognition</strong> with <strong>LLM-based response generation</strong> to conduct real-time phone conversations. Here is the simplified flow for both inbound and outbound calls:</p>
<div class="step-box">
<div class="step-list">
<div class="step-row"><div class="sn">1</div><div><h4>Call is initiated</h4><p><strong>Inbound:</strong> A patient dials your assigned DMEDesk number. <strong>Outbound:</strong> DMEDesk dials a list of contacts from your campaign (payers, physicians, patients).</p></div></div>
<div class="step-row"><div class="sn">2</div><div><h4>Speech Recognition</h4><p>The AI listens to what the caller says and transcribes it into text in real time using high-accuracy speech-to-text.</p></div></div>
<div class="step-row"><div class="sn">3</div><div><h4>LLM Decision</h4><p>DMEDesk uses your configured system prompt and knowledge base to craft the appropriate reply or decide the next action (verify patient, collect info, transfer call).</p></div></div>
<div class="step-row"><div class="sn">4</div><div><h4>Voice Output</h4><p>The AI's response is converted to natural-sounding speech by DMEDesk AI voice and played back to the caller in real time.</p></div></div>
<div class="step-row"><div class="sn">5</div><div><h4>Actions & Data</h4><p>After or during the call, intake details are prepared, transcripts are stored, and automations are triggered.</p></div></div>
</div>
</div>
<h2>Inbound vs. Outbound</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Inbound:</strong> Patient dials your number &rarr; AI picks up &rarr; greets, verifies, collects intake info &rarr; routes or escalates as needed.</li>
<li><strong>Outbound:</strong> You upload a lead list or sync from an approved source &rarr; AI dials each contact &rarr; follows your call script &rarr; logs outcome and triggers follow-up.</li>
</ul>

<div class="bc">Introduction <span>&rsaquo;</span> Key Terminology</div>
<div class="pg-title">Key Terminology</div>
<div class="pg-sub">Essential terms you need to know to use the DMEDesk platform effectively.</div>
<div class="term-grid">
<div class="term"><h3>&#129302; AI Receptionist / Agent</h3><p>The AI entity you configure to handle inbound or outbound calls. Defined by a system prompt, voice, and set of tools.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128227; Campaign (Outbound)</h3><p>A batch of outbound calls to a list of contacts &mdash; payers, physicians, or patients. Can be launched manually or triggered automatically.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#9742;&#65039; Inbound Number</h3><p>A dedicated phone number assigned to your AI receptionist. Patients call this number and the AI answers.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128221; System Prompt</h3><p>The instruction set that defines how your AI receptionist behaves &mdash; what it says, how it responds, what it collects, and when it escalates.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128202; Post-Call Variables</h3><p>Structured data extracted after each call (e.g., <code>patient_name</code>, <code>insurance_id</code>, <code>call_outcome</code>) used in automations and workflow handoffs.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128279; Workflow Integration</h3><p>A configurable handoff between DMEDesk and your operational workflow for routing patient records and call data.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128218; Knowledge Base</h3><p>Documents (PDFs, text files) your AI can reference during calls to answer questions about products, insurance, or processes.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128279; Webhook</h3><p>A URL endpoint that receives data when events occur (call ended, appointment booked) to trigger actions in external systems.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128737;&#65039; TPO Intake</h3><p>Treatment, Payment, and Operations intake &mdash; the HIPAA-compliant process for collecting patient information at the start of a new DME order.</p></div>
<div class="term"><h3>&#128274; BAA</h3><p>Business Associate Agreement &mdash; a HIPAA-required contract between DMEDesk and your organization that governs how PHI is handled.</p></div>

<div class="bc">Getting Started <span>&rsaquo;</span> Core Concepts</div>
<div class="pg-title">Core Concepts</div>
<div class="pg-sub">The building blocks of the DMEDesk platform.</div>
<h2>1. AI Receptionists</h2>
<p>At the heart of DMEDesk is the AI Receptionist &mdash; a configurable voice agent that handles phone calls on behalf of your DME practice. You define its behavior through a system prompt, select a voice, and connect it to your phone number and approved workflows.</p>
<h2>2. Phone Numbers</h2>
<p>Each AI Receptionist is assigned one or more phone numbers. These can be new numbers purchased through DMEDesk or existing numbers ported from your current carrier.</p>
<h2>3. Inbound vs. Outbound</h2>

<div class="pg-title">System Prompts</div>
<div class="pg-sub">The system prompt is the instruction set that defines your AI Receptionist's behavior.</div>
<h2>What is a System Prompt?</h2>
<p>The system prompt is a plain-text (or structured) instruction set you write to tell the AI how to behave during calls. It defines the greeting, what information to collect, how to handle edge cases, and when to transfer or end the call.</p>
<h2>Best Practices</h2>
<ul>
<li>Use <strong>objective-based prompting</strong> &mdash; tell the AI what outcome to achieve, not just what to say word for word.</li>
<li>Always include an <strong>AI disclosure</strong> in the opening line (required by law in many states).</li>
<li>Define clear <strong>escalation criteria</strong> &mdash; when should the AI transfer to a human?</li>
<li>Keep prompts focused. One AI per use case (intake, prior auth, patient notification) performs better than one AI trying to do everything.</li>
<li>Never include raw PHI in the system prompt itself.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Example Structure</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Identity:</strong> Who the AI is and what practice it represents</li>
<li><strong>Disclosure:</strong> AI identification statement</li>
<li><strong>Objective:</strong> What the AI should accomplish on this call</li>
<li><strong>Data Collection:</strong> What fields to gather from the caller</li>
<li><strong>Escalation Rules:</strong> When and how to transfer</li>
<li><strong>Closing:</strong> How to end the call professionally</li>
</ul>

<h2>AI goes off-script</h2>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> System prompt is too vague or doesn't cover the scenario the caller triggered.<br><strong>Fix:</strong> Add explicit instructions for the scenario. Use objective-based prompting and add guardrails: <em>"If the caller asks anything not related to [topic], politely redirect to [purpose]."</em></p>
<h2>AI collects wrong information</h2>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Field names or collection order in the prompt is ambiguous.<br><strong>Fix:</strong> Be explicit about each field name, format, and order. Example: <em>"Ask for the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format."</em></p>
<h2>AI doesn't transfer when it should</h2>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Transfer trigger conditions in the prompt are not specific enough.<br><strong>Fix:</strong> Add clear, specific transfer triggers. Example: <em>"If the caller says they want to speak to a human, immediately use the transfer tool."</em></p>
<h2>AI repeats itself</h2>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> The system prompt has conflicting instructions or the AI is looping on an unanswered question.<br><strong>Fix:</strong> Review for conflicting instructions. Add: <em>"If the caller does not answer after two attempts, offer to call them back and end the call."</em></p>
